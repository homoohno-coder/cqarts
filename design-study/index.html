With works, 2026 – 2017
The artist / Los Angeles

Figures meet, overlap and dissolve. Bodies move through spaces that refuse to stay still.

Christina Quarles is a Los Angeles-based artist working across painting, drawing and installation. Her practice explores identity, embodiment and the possibilities of the human figure.
